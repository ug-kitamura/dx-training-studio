# How to proceed with the training

## Learning goals

Technology evolves quickly, and you need to keep learning constantly. That's exactly why the most important thing we want you to take away from this training is **an attitude of learning for yourself, rather than being taught**. We'll do everything we can to support people who take that approach.

In this lesson, you'll learn the overall goal of the training and how to read the "Mandala," the map of your learning journey. By the time you finish reading, you'll be able to decide for yourself which order to proceed in.

## There's one goal—complete a working tool with your own hands

This training consists of this kickoff, three weeks of self-study, and finally two one-hour hands-on sessions. Let's state the main quest up front: **in the final hands-on session, you will build one small tool that helps with your work—with your own hands, and to the point where it actually runs.** That's the goal. You don't need to think about what to build right now. The organizers have prepared the theme, and it will be announced on the day of the hands-on session.

To get there, you'll gather your weapons during the three-week self-study period: how to ask AI questions, how to save and share code with Git and GitHub, and how to write scripts in Python. Every one of these has been chosen specifically because you'll actually use it on the day of the hands-on session.

When you finish, what you'll have in hand is a working tool you built yourself, and the experience of having researched, asked, and seen it through to completion. There's one thing we want you to promise: **when you don't understand something, look it up yourself first, then ask AI—and keep moving forward.** The organizers won't build the tool for you—because the ability to research things yourself and keep moving forward is exactly the way of working we want this training to instill in you.

## The map of your adventure—how to read the Mandala

The only thing worth paying attention to in the diagram is **where each arrow originates**. There are just two ways to read it that you need to remember.

1. **Finishing what's before an arrow unlocks what's after it.** The arrow to the AI Basics Series originates from the Intro to DX Course (you are here), and the arrows to the Git Basics Series and the Python Basics Series originate from the Development Environment Setup Course. In other words, to enter the Git Basics Series, you need to have finished the Development Environment Setup Course
2. **Where the path branches, you can go either way.** The map doesn't decide your order—you choose it yourself

You don't need to memorize the exact layout. It's enough to understand that there's a map you unlock as you go.

## Decide your own order

Looking at the map, the path branches into three directions after the Getting Started Series. You can move on to the AI Basics Series right away. The Git Basics Series and the Python Basics Series unlock once you finish the Development Environment Setup Course (the remaining two lessons in this series).

After that, you're free to proceed however you like. Some people push straight through Git and GitHub before starting Python; others finish Python first. **Once you complete every series, the path opens to the final Tool Development Practice Series (the hands-on sessions).**

Along the way, there are several points where you'll need to submit a request to set up your environment or to get access. While you're waiting for approval, keep moving forward on whatever you can.

## Where to turn when you're stuck

If you go it alone, you're bound to get stuck somewhere. That's why we've set up a Teams channel called **"DX Tools Training."** Every participant will be invited by the organizers.

- Questions are always welcome. That said, we may respond with **just a hint** rather than the answer itself—because room to think for yourself is one of your weapons
- The organizers and a few volunteers handle responses. Please understand that we may not always be able to reply right away

The order to follow is: first research it yourself and ask AI, and if you're still stuck, head to the channel. For things you have no way of resolving on your own—like the various requests covered at the kickoff, or problems with your environment—please don't hesitate to ask right away.

One more thing: these materials are updated even while the training is running. When there's a significant change, we'll announce it in the same channel, so take a look whenever you see one. What changed, and how, is recorded on the [changelog](../../changelog) page. To check whether the page you're reading is the latest, look at the update date at the very top of the sidebar.

## Summary

Here are the three things to take away.

1. The goal is to **complete one working tool with your own hands**
2. The Mandala is **a map you unlock as you go**. You choose your own order
3. If you get stuck, research it, ask AI, and if that still doesn't work, go to the **DX Tools Training** channel

Right now, what's unlocked is the Development Environment Setup Course and the AI Basics Series. Follow the order you just wrote out to your first quest. The adventure begins.
